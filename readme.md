# Zengen

**A simple way to keep adult content off your screen.**

Zengen is a browser extension that automatically blocks websites and pages containing adult content.

## What does Zengen do?

Zengen helps prevent you from accidentally or intentionally accessing adult content online.

It can:

- Block known adult websites
- Detect adult content on other websites
- Recognize content in multiple languages
- Work quickly without slowing down normal browsing
- Process content locally instead of sending it to an online service
- Detect content that appears after a page has loaded
